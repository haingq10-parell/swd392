# UC31: Correct Booking Charges

Source: CourtChain project report, use-case description UC31.

## Use Case
- **Actors:** Staff; Facility Manager (manager authorization actor).
- **Trigger:** During counter checkout, staff identifies an incorrect price calculation, unapplied voucher, or wrong surcharge and selects **Correct Charges**.
- **Preconditions:** The booking session is active or pending checkout; the user has permission to adjust billing.
- **Postconditions:** Corrected charges are reflected in the booking's payable summary; an audit record captures the adjustment reason, staff ID, and timestamp.

## Main Success Flow
1. Staff selects the booking record that needs correction.
2. The system loads the billing breakdown: court fee, equipment rental, service surcharges, and discounts.
3. Staff enters an adjustment amount or selects the correct pricing rule, and supplies a mandatory justification.
4. The system recalculates the net payable amount and updates the payment summary for review.
5. Staff reviews and confirms the corrected invoice.

## Alternative Flow
- **Manager authorization:** When the adjustment exceeds the staff limit, the system requests Facility Manager PIN/credential authorization. After approval, the correction continues.

## Exception Flow
- **Already settled:** If the booking is already PAID/COMPLETED, block direct correction and direct the operator to a refund or adjustment-voucher workflow.

## Business Rules
- **UC31 BR-41:** Staff may adjust booking item prices or apply manual discounts up to 200,000 VND without manager authorization.
- **UC31 BR-42:** Every manual adjustment records staff ID, timestamp, and justification for audit.
- **Receipt immutability:** The project report's global BR162 says issued receipt financial details cannot be directly modified; corrections require separate linked records. This is consistent with UC31's pre-settlement restriction, but the exact invoice/adjustment model must be confirmed against the implementation.
- Taxes and loyalty point earnings are to be recalculated after adjustment, but the report does not specify their formulas.

## Required Data
- Existing booking/session and its current charge lines: court fee, equipment rental, surcharges, discounts.
- Adjustment amount or selected pricing rule, mandatory reason, acting staff identity, timestamp, and manager authorization metadata where applicable.
- Updated payable total and any tax/loyalty recalculation supported by the existing project rules.
- The report's domain model includes Invoice, InvoiceDetail, Payment, and an Invoice self-reference for adjustments. Do not add tables until the actual schema is inspected.

## Clarifications Required Before Coding
1. Does the 200,000 VND limit apply to the absolute value of one correction, the total of all corrections on a booking, or only manual discounts? What is the permitted adjustment range (including increases)?
2. Which manager credential mechanism is actually implemented (PIN, password re-entry, or authenticated manager session), and must manager authorization be recorded as an auditable approval?
3. What formulas/services determine taxes and loyalty-point earnings? The report requires recalculation but gives no rates or rounding rules for this use case.
4. The report names BR-42 as the adjustment audit rule, while its global Business Rules section defines BR42 as cancelled-voucher consumption. Confirm the authoritative rule identifier/text.
5. The report describes the intended architecture as React/Vite + NestJS/TypeORM + PostgreSQL 16, but its database-design section also specifies SQL Server/T-SQL. The actual project source/configuration must determine which stack is in use.
